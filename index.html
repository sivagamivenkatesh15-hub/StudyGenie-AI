About the Project

StudyGenie AI is a full-stack, AI-powered study companion built with Flask, SQLite and the Google Gemini API. It combines syllabus analysis, adaptive scheduling and generative AI to help students plan, learn, and revise more effectively — all from one clean, responsive dashboard.
